    Lash aftercare

    Look after them and they'll look after you.

    1. Come to your appointment with clean, makeup-free eyes.
    2. Keep your lashes dry for the first 24 hours.
    3. Skip oil-based cleansers and mascara on your extensions.
    4. Brush them gently every morning with a clean spoolie.
    5. Sleep on your back or on a silk pillowcase where you can.
